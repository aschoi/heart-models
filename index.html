Heart models

Two interactive 3D teaching models of the human heart: one for anatomy, one for how the heart's electrical signal, its pumping and the ECG line up in time.

Heart anatomy model

Explore the chambers, valves and great vessels from any angle.

  • Exterior, cutaway and X-ray views
  • Clickable structures with descriptions
  • Animated blood flow through both circuits

Beating heart and ECG

Watch one heartbeat in slow motion, synced to its lead II ECG.

  • Conduction system pulse from SA node to Purkinje fibers
  • Valve states, heart sounds and ventricular volume
  • Playback speed from 0.05× to 2×, with scrubbing

Stylized teaching models built with Three.js, TypeScript and Vite. Geometry is simplified and timings are representative textbook values for a resting adult heart, not patient data.
